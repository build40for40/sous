# Mini Cranberry Meatballs

Bite-sized beef and Parmesan meatballs, browned and baked, then tossed in a tangy cranberry, ketchup and mustard glaze.

From [@buzzfeedtasty](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfVUc7PB1dv/) · caption

## Ingredients

- [ ] Meatballs: 1lb ground beef (80/20)
- [ ] 3/4 cup breadcrumbs
- [ ] 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- [ ] 1/4 cup shallot, finely chopped
- [ ] 2 cloves garlic, finely minced
- [ ] 1/4 cup canned whole berry cranberry sauce
- [ ] 1 large egg
- [ ] 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- [ ] 1 tsp dried oregano
- [ ] 1/2 tsp salt
- [ ] 1/2 tsp black pepper
- [ ] Sprinkle of fresh thyme
- [ ] Avocado oil
- [ ] Cranberry sauce: 2 cups canned whole berry cranberry sauce
- [ ] 1/2 cup ketchup
- [ ] 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- [ ] 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- [ ] 2 tsp Dijon mustard
- [ ] 3 tbsp brown sugar
- [ ] 1 tsp garlic powder
- [ ] 1 tsp onion powder
- [ ] 1/2 tsp black pepper
- [ ] Pinch of salt

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. In a large bowl, combine the beef, breadcrumbs, Parmesan, shallot, garlic, cranberry sauce, egg, Worcestershire sauce, oregano, salt and pepper. Mix until just combined without overworking.
3. Roll into mini meatballs about 1 inch wide.
4. Heat a shallow layer of avocado oil covering the base of a frying pan over medium-high heat. Brown the meatballs for about 1 minute per side.
5. Transfer to a baking tray lined with baking paper and bake for 7–9 minutes until cooked through, then set aside.
6. Meanwhile, put all the sauce ingredients in a saucepan and mix well. Cook over medium-low heat for 5–10 minutes, stirring now and then, until thickened.
7. Put the meatballs in a large bowl, pour over the sauce and stir gently to coat.
8. Tip onto a large platter and garnish with fresh thyme.

## Tips

- You can skip browning and bake the meatballs from raw for 15–20 minutes, until cooked through.
- If not serving straight away, keep the meatballs in a slow cooker with the sauce poured over.
