# Crispy Chicken Cutlet Focaccia Sandwiches

Panko-crumbed chicken cutlets pan-fried in olive oil and served in homemade focaccia.

From [@barefoodtim](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeHaGZ2On6H/) · video audio

## Ingredients

- [ ] Focaccia dough
- [ ] Olive oil
- [ ] Chicken cutlets
- [ ] Flour
- [ ] Egg mixture
- [ ] Panko breadcrumbs

## Method

1. Once the focaccia dough has risen, dimple it and bake it.
2. While it bakes, coat the chicken cutlets in flour, then the egg mixture, then panko breadcrumbs.
3. Pan-fry the cutlets in olive oil until crispy.
4. Fill the focaccia with the crispy chicken cutlets to make sandwiches.
