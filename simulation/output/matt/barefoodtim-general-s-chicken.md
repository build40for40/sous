# General's Chicken

Crispy air-fried breaded chicken bites tossed in a glossy, sweet and spicy soy, hoisin and ginger sauce.

From [@barefoodtim](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DehfZkuJzyt/) · caption

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 bag Just Bare lightly breaded chicken bites
- [ ] 1/3 cup low-sodium soy sauce
- [ ] 1/3 cup chicken stock or water
- [ ] 1/4 cup brown sugar
- [ ] 3 tbsp rice vinegar
- [ ] 2 tbsp hoisin sauce
- [ ] 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
- [ ] 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- [ ] 1 tbsp cornstarch (cornflour)
- [ ] 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
- [ ] 2 cloves garlic, finely grated
- [ ] 1-2 tsp chilli crisp or 4-6 dried red chillies
- [ ] Optional: 1 tsp MSG
- [ ] 1 tbsp neutral oil
- [ ] Sliced scallions (spring onions), to finish
- [ ] Sesame seeds, to finish

## Method

1. Whisk together the soy sauce, stock or water, brown sugar, rice vinegar, hoisin, Shaoxing wine, sesame oil, cornstarch, ginger, garlic, chilli crisp (if using) and MSG (if using). Keep any whole dried chillies aside.
2. Fry or air-fry the chicken bites until crisp.
3. Heat 1 tbsp neutral oil in a large frying pan or wok. If using dried chillies, toast them for about 20-30 seconds.
4. Pour in the sauce and simmer for 1-2 minutes, stirring, until glossy and thick enough to coat a spoon.
5. Add the crispy chicken and toss just long enough to coat it in the sauce.
6. Finish with sliced spring onions and sesame seeds.

## Tips

- Toss the chicken only briefly in the sauce so it stays crisp.
