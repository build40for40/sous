# Vegan Fruit Jelly Cake

Fresh grapes and strawberries set in a sparkling layer of juice or tea using agar instead of gelatine.

From [@buzzfeedtasty](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfVDUdOhvKe/) · caption + audio

## Ingredients

- [ ] Grapes
- [ ] Strawberries
- [ ] Juice or tea
- [ ] Agar agar powder
- [ ] Japanese whisky (optional, for flavour)

## Method

1. Arrange the fruit in a baking dish.
2. Cook the agar agar in the boiling juice or tea, adding the Japanese whisky for flavour if using.
3. Let the liquid cool slightly, then pour it over the fruit.
4. Chill in the fridge; it sets within minutes.

## Tips

- Agar must be cooked in boiling liquid to set.
- Swap in your favourite fruits and flavours.
