# Banana Coconut Cookies with Chocolate Drizzle

Two-ingredient cookies of mashed banana and shredded coconut, baked until golden and finished with an optional melted chocolate drizzle.

From [@feelgoodfoodie](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DTwZpo5DNVr/) · caption · serves 12-13 cookies

## Ingredients

- [ ] 2 ripe bananas
- [ ] 2 cups unsweetened shredded coconut
- [ ] ½ cup chocolate chips (optional, for drizzling)

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 350°F and line a baking sheet with baking parchment.
2. Mash the bananas in a large bowl, add the shredded coconut and mix with a fork until fully combined.
3. Scoop rounded balls of the mixture with a 1.5 tablespoon cookie scoop onto the lined sheet, spacing them about 2 inches apart.
4. Bake for 12-15 minutes, until the cookies look set and the coconut is golden.
5. Meanwhile, microwave the chocolate chips in a small bowl in 20-second bursts, stirring each time, until smooth and runny.
6. Drizzle the melted chocolate over the baked cookies and chill for 30 minutes.

## Tips

- You can mix the bananas and coconut in a blender instead of with a fork.
- Flatten the balls slightly before baking.
- The cookies are finished without the chocolate; the drizzle is optional.
