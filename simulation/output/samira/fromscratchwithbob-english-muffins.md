# English Muffins

Homemade yeasted English muffins dusted in semolina and cooked in a dry frying pan or skillet.

From [@fromscratchwithbob](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DclEvxoORVU/) · caption

## Ingredients

- [ ] 350 ml milk
- [ ] 550 grams bread flour
- [ ] 1 tbsp caster sugar
- [ ] 7 grams dried yeast
- [ ] 1 tsp salt
- [ ] Semolina (for dusting)

## Method

1. Warm the milk gently in a saucepan over a low heat until just warm, then stir in the yeast.
2. Mix the flour, sugar and salt in a bowl, then pour in the warm milk.
3. Mix to a dough, then knead for 10 minutes until smooth and elastic.
4. Roll the dough out on a floured surface to about 2-2.5cm thick.
5. Cut out rounds with a 7.5cm cutter, re-rolling the offcuts until all the dough is used.
6. Dust a tray with semolina, arrange the muffins on it without touching, and dust the tops with more semolina.
7. Cover loosely and leave somewhere warm for 1 hour, or until doubled in size.
8. Heat a large frying pan or skillet over a medium heat and cook the muffins in batches, without oil or butter, for about 7 minutes per side until firm and golden brown.
9. Cool on a wire rack and eat warm, or freeze once cool.

## Tips

- Any cutter of a similar size will do.
- The semolina stops them sticking, so no fat is needed in the pan.
- If the bottoms brown too quickly, turn the heat down.
