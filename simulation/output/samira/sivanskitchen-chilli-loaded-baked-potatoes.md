# Chilli-Loaded Baked Potatoes

Salt-crusted, crispy-skinned baked russet potatoes split open and loaded with a quick tomato, beef and kidney bean chilli and fresh toppings.

From [@sivanskitchen](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfTG7aHv1Wn/) · caption · serves 4

## Ingredients

- [ ] Potatoes: 4 russet potatoes, washed and scrubbed
- [ ] 3 cups water
- [ ] 2 tbsp salt
- [ ] About 3 tbsp olive oil, for brushing
- [ ] Chilli: 3 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 5 ripe cluster tomatoes, tops removed
- [ ] 1 large onion, chopped
- [ ] 1 lb ground beef (85/15)
- [ ] 4 garlic cloves, chopped
- [ ] 1/3 cup tomato paste
- [ ] 1 cup canned kidney beans, rinsed and drained
- [ ] 1 tbsp paprika
- [ ] 2–3 tsp salt, to taste
- [ ] 1 tsp black pepper
- [ ] 2 tsp ground cumin
- [ ] Toppings: sliced ripe avocado
- [ ] Diced onion
- [ ] Chopped coriander (cilantro)
- [ ] Sliced serrano chillies
- [ ] Flaky salt

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 450°F (230°C). Stir the salt into the water in a bowl.
2. Roll the scrubbed potatoes in the salt bath, then set them on a wire rack over a baking tray. Bake for 1 hour.
3. Meanwhile, heat 3 tbsp olive oil in a 4-quart pot over medium heat. Lay the tomatoes in cut-side down, put the onion in the middle and cover. Cook for about 10 minutes until the tomato skins loosen, giving it another 3–5 minutes covered if needed.
4. Peel off the tomato skins and mash the tomatoes well.
5. Add the beef, garlic, paprika, salt, pepper and cumin. Break up the beef and mix well, then stir in the tomato paste and beans.
6. Cover and cook for about 10 minutes, stirring now and then, until the beef is cooked through.
7. Once the potatoes have had 1 hour, brush the skins with olive oil and return them to the oven for 10 more minutes until crisp outside and tender inside.
8. Split the potatoes lengthwise, open them up and fluff the insides with a fork. Spoon over the chilli and finish with your toppings.

## Tips

- In the video the creator pokes the potatoes with a fork before rolling them in the salt bath.
- Rolling the potatoes in salted water gives the skins an even coating of salt.
- The creator leaves out cheese to keep it kosher; add whatever toppings you like.
- Good for meal prep or as a filling post-workout meal.
