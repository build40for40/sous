# Greek Lemon Chicken and Potatoes with Greek Salad

Bone-in chicken and potatoes baked in a lemon, garlic, Dijon and oregano sauce, served with a dairy-free Greek salad.

From [@sivanskitchen](https://www.instagram.com/reel/De-ZeM9POHt/) · caption

## Ingredients

- [ ] Chicken & potatoes:
- [ ] 3 lbs golden potatoes, peeled and quartered
- [ ] 7 bone-in chicken pieces (about 2 lbs 10 oz)
- [ ] Sauce:
- [ ] 1/2 cup fresh lemon juice (about 2 large lemons)
- [ ] 1/4 cup olive oil
- [ ] 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- [ ] 6 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 2–3 tsp salt
- [ ] A few fresh oregano sprigs or 1 tbsp dried
- [ ] 1/4 cup water
- [ ] Dairy-free Greek salad:
- [ ] 6 Persian cucumbers
- [ ] 3 beefsteak tomatoes
- [ ] 1 red pepper
- [ ] 1/2 red onion
- [ ] 1 cup pitted Kalamata olives
- [ ] 1 bunch flat-leaf parsley
- [ ] Dressing:
- [ ] 1/3 cup olive oil
- [ ] 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice
- [ ] 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- [ ] 2 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 1 tbsp dried oregano
- [ ] 2 tsp salt
- [ ] 1 tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 190°C (375°F).
2. Pat the chicken dry and lay it in a large ovenproof pot or braiser, then put the potatoes on top.
3. Put all the sauce ingredients except the water in a jar and shake well. Pour over the chicken and potatoes.
4. Add the water to the jar, swirl to pick up the rest of the sauce and pour it into the pot.
5. Cover with an ovenproof lid, or baking parchment and foil, and bake for 1½ hours.
6. Uncover, baste with the juices and bake for another 30 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the potatoes are tender and golden.
7. Meanwhile, chop the salad vegetables and parsley and mix them with the olives in a bowl.
8. Shake the dressing ingredients in a clean jar.
9. Toss the salad with the dressing just before serving, and serve with the chicken and potatoes.

## Tips

- Any chicken pieces work, bone-in or boneless.
- Only dress the salad when you're ready to serve.
