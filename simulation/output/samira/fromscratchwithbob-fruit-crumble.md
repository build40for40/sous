# Fruit Crumble

A simple crumble of any soft fruit under a rubbed-in topping of flour, butter and sugar in a 2:1:1 ratio.

From [@fromscratchwithbob](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Da2OSYjOvrB/) · caption

## Ingredients

- [ ] Soft fruit (enough to fill the baking dish about two thirds full)
- [ ] 240g plain flour
- [ ] 120g cold butter
- [ ] 120g caster sugar

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan).
2. Fill a baking dish about two thirds full with the soft fruit, leaving room for the topping.
3. Put the flour, butter and sugar in a mixing bowl and rub them together with your fingertips until they look like fine crumbs.
4. Scatter the topping over the fruit, squeezing some of it together to make chunks.
5. Bake for about 20 minutes, until the top is golden brown and the fruit is bubbling underneath.
6. Serve warm with cream, ice cream or custard.

## Tips

- The topping ratio is 2 parts flour to 1 part butter to 1 part sugar; scale it up for a bigger dish.
- Leftover topping keeps in the fridge for later.
- Swap some of the flour for oats for more texture.
- Use brown sugar for a more golden topping, or reduce the sugar for a less sweet crumble.
- If the top browns before the fruit is cooked, cover loosely with foil and keep baking.
- Fresh or frozen fruit both work.
