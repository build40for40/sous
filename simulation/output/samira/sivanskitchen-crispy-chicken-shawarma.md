# Crispy Chicken Shawarma

Marinated chicken stacked on skewers between onion halves and roasted like a shawarma spit, served in crispy pan-toasted pitta with tahini and sumac onions.

From [@sivanskitchen](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfVr002PvI-/) · caption

## Ingredients

- [ ] Chicken: 2 lb boneless chicken thighs, breasts or a mix, pounded thin
- [ ] Marinade: 1/4 cup lemon juice
- [ ] 1/4 cup olive oil
- [ ] 1/4 cup hot pepper paste
- [ ] 1 tbsp shawarma spice
- [ ] 2 tsp salt
- [ ] 1 tsp black pepper
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 2 wooden skewers
- [ ] 1 large onion, cut in half across the middle
- [ ] 1/2 cup water (if needed while baking)
- [ ] Tahini sauce: 1 cup tahini
- [ ] Juice of 1 lemon
- [ ] 1–2 tsp salt
- [ ] About 3/4 cup ice-cold water, as needed
- [ ] Onion sumac salad: 1 large onion, sliced
- [ ] Juice of 1 lemon
- [ ] Drizzle of olive oil
- [ ] 1 tsp salt
- [ ] 2 tsp sumac
- [ ] To serve: 1 large pitta (split) or lavash
- [ ] Pickles (optional)
- [ ] Shawarma pan drippings, or 1 tbsp hot pepper paste mixed with 2 tbsp oil
- [ ] Pomegranate molasses

## Method

1. Mix all the marinade ingredients in your baking tin, add the chicken and coat well.
2. Set one onion half cut-side down and push the 2 skewers into it about 2 inches apart.
3. Thread the chicken onto the skewers, alternating thigh and breast, then press the second onion half on top. Stand it back in the tin.
4. Bake at 400°F (200°C) for about 1 hour, basting as it cooks. Add 1/2 cup water to the tin once or twice if needed.
5. Meanwhile, whisk the tahini, lemon juice and salt, adding the ice-cold water gradually until smooth.
6. Toss the sliced onion with the lemon juice, olive oil, salt and sumac.
7. Slice the shawarma thinly. Fill the pitta with chicken, onion salad and pickles and wrap it up.
8. Brush the outside with the drippings (or the pepper paste and oil mix) and toast seam-side down in a pan over medium heat, turning until crisp on all sides.
9. Finish with tahini sauce and pomegranate molasses.

## Tips

- If the skewer stack falls over, bake it on its side and turn it every 10 minutes.
- Use a meat thermometer: the chicken should reach 165°F (74°C), or 170°F for extra safety.
