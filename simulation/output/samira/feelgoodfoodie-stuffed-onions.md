# Stuffed Onions

Onion layers rolled around a Middle Eastern-spiced beef and rice filling, baked in a tomato and pomegranate molasses sauce.

From [@feelgoodfoodie](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DVKgkKPDCjr/) · recipe page · serves 8 · 1 hr 45 mins

## Ingredients

- [ ] 5 large yellow onions
- [ ] Water (for boiling)
- [ ] 1 pound 85% lean ground beef
- [ ] 1 cup short-grain rice, rinsed and drained
- [ ] 2 Roma tomatoes, finely chopped
- [ ] ½ cup chopped fresh parsley
- [ ] 2 teaspoons 7 Spice
- [ ] 2 teaspoons coriander
- [ ] 1½ teaspoons salt (filling)
- [ ] 1 teaspoon black pepper
- [ ] 2 cups water (sauce)
- [ ] ¼ cup olive oil
- [ ] 3 tablespoons tomato paste
- [ ] 2 tablespoons pomegranate molasses
- [ ] 1 teaspoon salt (sauce)

## Method

1. Bring a large pan of water to the boil.
2. Trim both ends off the onions and cut a shallow slit down one side into the centre.
3. Boil the onions for 8-10 minutes until slightly tender, then drain and leave to cool.
4. Carefully peel the onions apart into separate layers, keeping the innermost 2-3 layers for another use.
5. In a large bowl, mix the beef, rice, tomatoes, parsley, 7 Spice, coriander, 1½ teaspoons salt and the pepper until just combined.
6. Put 1-2 tablespoons of filling into each onion layer and roll it up loosely, leaving room for the rice to swell.
7. Heat the oven to 400°F.
8. Pack the stuffed onions seam-side down in a single snug layer in a wide ovenproof pan with a lid.
9. Whisk the 2 cups water, olive oil, tomato paste, pomegranate molasses and 1 teaspoon salt together and pour it evenly over the onions.
10. Bake covered for 60 minutes, then uncover and bake for another 15 minutes; grill for 2-3 minutes if you want more colour.
11. Rest for 10 minutes, then serve with extra sauce spooned over.

## Tips

- Each onion should give about 6-7 layers for stuffing.
- Don't roll the onions too tightly, as the rice needs room to expand.
