# Zucchini Brownies

Rich, fudgy one-bowl chocolate brownies made with grated zucchini (courgette) and chocolate chips.

From [@feelgoodfoodie](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfORo6hRYny/) · recipe page · serves 16 pieces · 45 minutes

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 1/4 cups granulated sugar
- [ ] 1/2 cup avocado oil
- [ ] 1 large egg (room temperature)
- [ ] 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- [ ] 1 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- [ ] 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
- [ ] 1/4 teaspoon salt
- [ ] 1 cup finely grated zucchini (courgette), packed
- [ ] 1/2 cup chocolate chips

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and line an 8x8-inch baking tin with baking parchment.
2. Whisk the sugar and oil together in a large bowl until well combined, then whisk in the egg and vanilla until smooth.
3. Add the cocoa powder, flour and salt and stir with a spatula until mostly combined; the batter will be very thick.
4. Fold in the courgette and leave the batter for 2-3 minutes so the courgette releases moisture and loosens it, then stir again until combined.
5. Fold in the chocolate chips and spread the batter evenly in the tin.
6. Bake for 30 minutes, until the edges are set and a cocktail stick inserted in the centre comes out with a few moist crumbs.
7. Cool completely on a wire rack before cutting into 16 squares.

## Tips

- If your courgette is very wet, add a little extra flour.
