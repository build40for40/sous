# Everything Sauce

A creamy, herby green sauce of mayonnaise, yoghurt, fresh herbs, jalapeño and lime blended with olive oil.

From [@nick.digiovanni](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DZISw_yNLQq/) · video audio

## Ingredients

- [ ] ⅓ cup mayonnaise
- [ ] ⅓ cup Greek yoghurt
- [ ] 1 cup fresh coriander (cilantro)
- [ ] ½ cup fresh basil
- [ ] ¼ cup fresh mint and parsley
- [ ] 2 jalapeños, seeds removed
- [ ] 1 tablespoon aji amarillo paste
- [ ] Zest and juice of 1 lime
- [ ] 1 teaspoon white wine vinegar
- [ ] 1 teaspoon honey
- [ ] ¼ teaspoon Dijon mustard
- [ ] Pinch of salt
- [ ] ¼ cup olive oil

## Method

1. Put the mayonnaise, yoghurt, coriander, basil, mint, parsley, jalapeños, aji amarillo paste, lime zest and juice, vinegar, honey, mustard and salt into a blender.
2. Blend, drizzling in the olive oil while the blender runs, until smooth.

## Tips

- It is spoken as "a quarter cup fresh mint and parsley", which could mean ¼ cup of each.
