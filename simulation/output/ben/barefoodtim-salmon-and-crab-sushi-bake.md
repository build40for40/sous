# Salmon and Crab Sushi Bake

A baked sushi casserole of rice and nori layered with smoked salmon, crab and spicy Japanese mayo.

From [@barefoodtim](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfU2Kq3JyMR/) · video audio

## Ingredients

- [ ] Salmon
- [ ] Brown sugar
- [ ] Soy sauce
- [ ] Crab
- [ ] Spicy Japanese mayo (Bachan's)
- [ ] Rice
- [ ] Nori
- [ ] Bachan's sauce
- [ ] Mayo
- [ ] Garnish of choice

## Method

1. Smoke the salmon with brown sugar and soy sauce for about 1 hour.
2. Make the filling by combining the salmon, crab and spicy Japanese mayo.
3. Layer the rice, nori and filling in a baking dish.
4. Top with more Bachan's sauce and mayo.
5. Bake, then garnish and serve.

## Tips

- The spicy mayo contains rice vinegar, which suits the rice.
