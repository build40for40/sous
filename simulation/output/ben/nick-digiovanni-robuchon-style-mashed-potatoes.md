# Robuchon-Style Mashed Potatoes

Ultra-smooth, very buttery mashed potatoes in the style of Joël Robuchon.

From [@nick.digiovanni](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DY7at0aNs4S/) · video audio

## Ingredients

- [ ] 2 pounds Yukon Gold potatoes
- [ ] Salted water (for boiling)
- [ ] 1 pound cold butter, cubed
- [ ] ½ cup warm milk
- [ ] Pinch of salt

## Method

1. Boil the potatoes in salted water until tender when pierced with a fork.
2. Peel them while still hot, using a clean tea towel to protect your hands.
3. Push the potatoes through a food mill or fine sieve for a very smooth texture.
4. Put the purée over a low heat and beat in the cold butter a few cubes at a time.
5. Finish by drizzling in the warm milk and adding a pinch of salt, stirring until silky.

## Tips

- Plating with a spoon swirl, Robuchon-style, is optional.
