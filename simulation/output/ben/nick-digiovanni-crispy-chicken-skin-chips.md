# Crispy Chicken Skin Chips

Paper-thin, glass-like crisps made by baking scraped chicken skin pressed flat between baking sheets.

From [@nick.digiovanni](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYpZA9INHng/) · video audio · About 1 hour baking

## Ingredients

- [ ] Chicken skins
- [ ] Salt

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 285°F.
2. Scrape all the fat from the chicken skins until they are thin enough to see light through.
3. Cut the skins into whatever shapes and sizes you like.
4. Lay them out evenly on a baking sheet lined with baking parchment and season lightly with salt.
5. Cover with a second sheet of parchment and weigh it down with another baking sheet.
6. Bake for about 1 hour, until very thin and crisp.

## Tips

- Pressing the skins under a second baking sheet keeps them flat and extra thin.
