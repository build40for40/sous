# Sheet Pan Beef Kebabs with Roasted Vegetables and Fluffy White Rice

Spiced minced beef kebabs baked flat on a sheet pan under a rack of roasting peppers, tomatoes and onions, served with fluffy basmati rice.

From [@sivanskitchen](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Depy8xmvEGE/) · caption

## Ingredients

- [ ] Kebabs: 1 lb ground beef (85/15), or ground meat of choice
- [ ] Kebabs: 1 onion
- [ ] Kebabs: 1 red bell pepper
- [ ] Kebabs: 1 bunch parsley
- [ ] Kebabs: 3 garlic cloves
- [ ] Kebabs: 3 tsp paprika in oil
- [ ] Kebabs: 2 tsp salt
- [ ] Kebabs: 1 tsp black pepper
- [ ] Kebabs: 1/4 cup tomato paste
- [ ] Kebabs: 2 tbsp oil
- [ ] Vegetables: red peppers
- [ ] Vegetables: Anaheim peppers
- [ ] Vegetables: jalapeños (optional)
- [ ] Vegetables: tomatoes on the vine
- [ ] Vegetables: onions, quartered
- [ ] Vegetables: olive oil
- [ ] Vegetables: salt
- [ ] Rice: 2 cups basmati rice, washed and drained
- [ ] Rice: 3 1/2 cups water (4 cups for softer rice)
- [ ] Rice: about 3 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] Rice: 2 tsp salt

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 425°F.
2. Blitz the onion, red pepper, parsley and garlic in a food processor until very finely chopped. Tip into a fine sieve and squeeze out as much liquid as you can.
3. Mix the squeezed vegetables with the beef, paprika, salt, pepper, tomato paste and oil until evenly combined.
4. Spread the mixture evenly over a half sheet pan, about half an inch thick. Cut it into strips about 1 1/2 inches wide with a knife or bench scraper, then score across to make portions.
5. Set an elevated wire rack over the meat and arrange the vegetables on it. Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with salt.
6. Bake for 15 minutes, check, then bake for about 7 minutes more until the kebabs are cooked through. Grill for up to 5 minutes for extra colour, watching closely. If the vegetables are done first, lift off the rack and give the kebabs another 5 minutes as needed.
7. Carefully spoon off any excess juices from the pan and keep them for serving.
8. Meanwhile, put the rice, water, olive oil and salt in a good non-stick pot, stir and bring to the boil uncovered.
9. When the water has dropped to the level of the rice, cover, turn the heat to low and simmer for about 10 minutes.
10. Turn off the heat and leave the rice to rest, covered, for 5 minutes, then fluff it up.
11. Serve the kebabs, vegetables and rice together on a platter.

## Tips

- A sheet pan with an elevated wire rack lets the vegetables roast directly above the kebabs.
- Some juices in the pan are normal; spoon them off and serve them alongside.
- The kebabs also work well in pitta instead of with rice.
- Leftovers are even better the next day.
