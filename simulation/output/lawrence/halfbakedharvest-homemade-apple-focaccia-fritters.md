# Homemade Apple Focaccia Fritters

Soft focaccia-style cider dough wrapped around cinnamon-sugar Honeycrisp apples, fried until crisp and dunked in a sweet-salty vanilla espresso glaze.

From [@halfbakedharvest](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfQ0z7wB0Wv/) · recipe page · serves 12 fritters · 2 hours

## Ingredients

- [ ] Dough: 1 1/2 cups warm apple cider
- [ ] 2 1/4 teaspoons active dry or instant yeast
- [ ] 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- [ ] 2 tablespoons salted butter, softened
- [ ] 1 egg
- [ ] 4 cups all-purpose flour, plus a little for sprinkling
- [ ] 1 teaspoon salt
- [ ] Apples: 3 cups Honeycrisp apples, diced (about 3 apples)
- [ ] 1/2 cup brown sugar
- [ ] 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- [ ] 1 tablespoon molasses
- [ ] 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- [ ] Glaze: 3 cups powdered sugar
- [ ] 1/4 cup warm whole milk, plus extra if needed
- [ ] 1/4 cup real maple syrup
- [ ] 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- [ ] 1 tablespoon instant coffee (or a shot of espresso)
- [ ] Flaky sea salt
- [ ] About 1 quart avocado oil, for frying
- [ ] Parchment baking cups

## Method

1. In a stand mixer fitted with the dough hook, combine the warm cider, yeast and sugar. Leave for 5–10 minutes until bubbly.
2. Add the butter, egg, flour and salt and knead for about 4 minutes until smooth. Cover and leave to rise for 1 hour, or until doubled.
3. Meanwhile, put the apples, brown sugar, cinnamon and molasses in a frying pan over medium heat. Cook, stirring now and then, for 5–6 minutes until the apples soften and the mixture is bubbly and syrupy, then cook for 1 more minute.
4. Take the apples off the heat, tip into a heatproof bowl, stir in the vanilla and leave to cool.
5. Knock back the dough and roll it into a large oval about 1/2 inch thick. Spoon the apples over one half, sprinkle lightly with flour, fold the dough over and seal the edges, then press gently into a long oval.
6. Make 10–12 cuts across the dough, then 5–6 cuts the other way to make a crosshatch. Pull the pieces apart into individual fritters, tucking in any loose apple.
7. Put each fritter in a parchment baking cup, cover and leave to rest while the oil heats.
8. Heat about 1 quart of avocado oil in a large Dutch oven to 350°F, filling the pot no more than a third full.
9. Fry the fritters in batches, lowering them in with their parchment cups. Fry for 2 minutes, flip, remove the parchment with tongs, then fry for another 1–2 minutes until deep golden and cooked through. Drain on a cooling rack.
10. Whisk the powdered sugar, warm milk, maple syrup, vanilla and instant coffee together, add a pinch of flaky sea salt, and loosen with a little more milk if needed.
11. Dunk the warm fritters in the glaze or drizzle it generously over the top, let the excess drip off, and serve.

## Tips

- The creator also sprinkles a little extra cinnamon sugar over the apples before folding the dough.
- Brown parchment paper liners make the fritters much easier to lower into and lift out of the oil.
- Shaping is messy; the fritters don't need to look perfect.
- Best eaten fresh.
