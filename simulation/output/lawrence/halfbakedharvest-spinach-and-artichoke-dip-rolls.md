# Spinach and Artichoke Dip Rolls

Flaky puff pastry rolled around a cheesy spinach and artichoke dip, topped with pretzel salt and finished with sage brown butter.

From [@halfbakedharvest](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfV_arWgXno/) · recipe page · serves 16 rolls · 1 hour

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 container (12 ounce) frozen chopped spinach, thawed and squeezed of excess water
- [ ] 1 cup marinated artichokes, chopped
- [ ] 4 ounces cream cheese, at room temperature
- [ ] 1 cup shredded provolone cheese
- [ ] 1 cup shredded gruyere cheese
- [ ] 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- [ ] 1 teaspoon onion powder
- [ ] 1 teaspoon black pepper
- [ ] Chilli flakes
- [ ] 2 sheets (1 box) frozen puff pastry, thawed
- [ ] 1 egg yolk, beaten with 1 tablespoon water
- [ ] Pretzel salt, for sprinkling
- [ ] 4 tablespoons salted butter
- [ ] 12 fresh sage leaves

## Method

1. Line a baking tray with baking paper.
2. In a bowl, mix the spinach, artichokes, cream cheese, provolone, gruyere, garlic powder, onion powder, pepper and chilli flakes until combined.
3. Roll each pastry sheet into a slightly larger rectangle, then cut each in half lengthwise to make 4 pieces in total.
4. Spoon a quarter of the filling along one long edge of each piece. Brush the opposite edge with the egg wash, roll the pastry over the filling, pinch to seal and place seam-side down on the tray.
5. Brush the tops with egg wash, sprinkle with pretzel salt and freeze for 20 minutes.
6. Preheat the oven to 425°F and bake for 20 minutes, until deep golden brown.
7. Meanwhile, brown the butter in a small pan and stir in the sage leaves.
8. Spoon the sage butter and leaves over the rolls and serve warm, broken in half.

## Tips

- The pretzel salt is what the creator says makes these special.
