# Maple Banana and Pecan Egg White Oats

Creamy, high-protein jumbo oat porridge thickened with egg whites and topped with Greek yoghurt and maple-caramelised banana and pecans.

From [@emthenutritionist](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfXpO7TokQv/) · caption · serves 1

## Ingredients

- [ ] 35g jumbo oats
- [ ] 100ml semi-skimmed milk, plus a splash if needed
- [ ] 75ml water
- [ ] 100ml cartoned egg whites
- [ ] Pinch of salt
- [ ] 1/2 small banana, sliced
- [ ] 1 tsp maple syrup
- [ ] A little butter
- [ ] 3 pecan halves
- [ ] 100g Greek yoghurt or skyr, to serve
- [ ] Nut butter, to finish (optional)

## Method

1. Put the oats, milk, water and a pinch of salt in a small saucepan. Simmer gently for 10–12 minutes, stirring now and then, until the oats are soft and most of the liquid is absorbed.
2. Turn the heat right down and gradually stir in the egg whites, stirring constantly for 2–3 minutes until cooked, thick and creamy. Add a splash of milk only if needed.
3. Meanwhile, melt a little butter with the maple syrup in a small frying pan. Add the banana and pecans and cook for 2–3 minutes until golden and caramelised.
4. Spoon the oats into a bowl, add a generous dollop of Greek yoghurt and top with the warm banana and pecans.
5. Finish with a little nut butter if you like.

## Tips

- Around 390kcal and 30g protein per serving, according to the creator.
- If you only have regular oats, the creator suggests stirring in a tablespoon of chia or flax seeds.
