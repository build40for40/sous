# Creamy Chicken, Leek and Pancetta Pie

A creamy chicken, leek, pancetta and pea filling with tarragon, topped with a golden puff pastry lid.

From [@emthenutritionist](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeruSjcoJvE/) · caption · serves 4

## Ingredients

- [ ] 300g cooked shredded chicken
- [ ] 500ml semi-skimmed milk
- [ ] 2 celery sticks, finely diced
- [ ] 120g frozen peas
- [ ] 2 leeks, finely sliced
- [ ] 2 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- [ ] 6 rashers smoked pancetta, diced
- [ ] 1 chicken stock cube
- [ ] 1 tbsp fresh chopped tarragon
- [ ] 1 big heaped tbsp plain flour
- [ ] Approx. 250g ready-rolled puff pastry, or enough to cover your dish
- [ ] 1 egg, beaten, for brushing
- [ ] Salt and plenty of black pepper

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 190°C fan.
2. Fry the diced pancetta in a deep pan for 3-4 minutes until it starts to turn golden and release its fat.
3. Add the leeks and celery, season with salt and plenty of black pepper, cover and cook gently for 8-10 minutes until soft. Stir in the garlic for the last minute.
4. Sprinkle over the flour and mix well, then slowly pour in the milk, stirring as you go.
5. Crumble in the stock cube, add the Dijon mustard and bring to a gentle simmer until thickened and creamy.
6. Fold in the peas, chicken and tarragon and let everything bubble together for a few minutes. Check the seasoning, then leave to cool slightly.
7. Tip the filling into your pie dish. Unroll the pastry and cut it to fit the dish.
8. Lay the pastry over the filling, crimp the edges and cut a small air vent in the middle.
9. Brush generously with beaten egg and bake for 25-30 minutes until puffed and golden and the filling is piping hot.
10. Serve with plenty of greens.

## Tips

- Press the dish or its lid onto the pastry to mark out the perfect shape before cutting.
- Let the filling cool slightly before adding the pastry lid.
- Decorate the pastry however you like before baking.
