# Easy Pot Roast Stroganoff

Chuck roast slow-cooked with mushrooms, onion, thyme, broth and wine, shredded and stirred with Greek yogurt, served over buttered egg noodles.

From [@halfbakedharvest](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfI_BlsBHj8/) · recipe page · serves 6 · 4 hours 15 minutes

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 (3-4 pound) beef chuck roast
- [ ] Salt and black pepper
- [ ] 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- [ ] 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- [ ] 2 tablespoons flour
- [ ] 1 yellow onion, chopped
- [ ] 3-4 cups quartered mushrooms
- [ ] 2 tablespoons chopped fresh thyme
- [ ] 4 cups broth
- [ ] 1/2 cup dry white wine
- [ ] 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- [ ] 1 tablespoon dijon mustard
- [ ] 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt or sour cream
- [ ] 1/2 cup fresh parsley, chopped
- [ ] 8 ounces uncooked egg noodles or pasta
- [ ] 3 tablespoons salted butter

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 325°F.
2. Put the roast in a large ovenproof casserole dish. Season with salt and pepper, then rub all over with the garlic powder, cayenne and flour.
3. Tuck the onion, mushrooms and thyme around the roast. Pour the broth, wine and Worcestershire sauce around it and add the Dijon.
4. Cover and roast for 3 1/2 to 5 hours, until very tender.
5. Set the casserole over a low heat on the hob. Add a little more broth if needed so the onions are just covered.
6. Shred the meat, then stir in the yogurt until combined. Season with salt and pepper and top with parsley.
7. Cook the noodles and drain. Toss the hot noodles with the salted butter.
8. Serve the stroganoff over bowls of buttered noodles, topped with more parsley.

## Tips

- Slow cooker version: prepare the roast the same way in the slow cooker, cover and cook on low for 5-6 hours or high for 3-4 hours, then shred, stir in the yogurt and keep on low until serving.
- Serve with crusty bread and a salad on the side.
