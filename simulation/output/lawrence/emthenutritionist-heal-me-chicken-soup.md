# 'Heal Me' Chicken Soup

A nourishing chicken, vegetable and brown rice soup made silky with an egg and lemon finish and fresh herbs.

From [@emthenutritionist](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DewzcpmoEDP/) · caption · serves 4

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 2 carrots, diced
- [ ] 200g diced butternut squash
- [ ] 2 sticks celery, diced
- [ ] 1 large onion, finely chopped
- [ ] A handful of chestnut mushrooms, sliced
- [ ] 1 tsp ginger paste
- [ ] 2 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 2 chicken stock cubes dissolved in 2.5L boiling water
- [ ] 1 small sprig rosemary
- [ ] 150g brown rice
- [ ] 1 lemon (zest and juice)
- [ ] 2 eggs
- [ ] 320-400g cooked roast chicken, shredded
- [ ] 1 tbsp each chopped dill and parsley
- [ ] Salt and plenty of black pepper

## Method

1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over a medium heat. Add the onion, carrots, butternut squash and celery with a pinch of salt and sauté for 5 minutes.
2. Add the mushrooms and cook until softened.
3. Stir in the ginger, garlic, rosemary and rice and cook for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
4. Pour in 2 litres of the hot stock, keeping the remaining ~500ml back for topping up, and bring to a simmer.
5. Cover and cook for 20 minutes, stirring now and then and adding reserved stock as needed to keep it soupy.
6. Stir in the chicken and lemon zest and simmer for 5 minutes, or until the rice is cooked. Remove the rosemary and turn the heat to low.
7. Whisk the eggs with the lemon juice in a bowl, then slowly whisk in a ladle or two of hot broth to temper them.
8. Gradually stir the egg mixture back into the pot, stirring constantly, until the soup turns silky. Turn off the heat.
9. Season with salt and plenty of black pepper, fold through the dill and parsley and serve hot.

## Tips

- Top with a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil and extra herbs.
- Leftovers store and reheat well.
