# 15-Minute Harissa Lamb Flatbread

A warm flatbread spread with houmous and layered with rocket, Moroccan-style couscous, harissa-spiced lamb steak, a garlic, lemon and mint yoghurt sauce, pomegranate seeds and fresh mint.

From [@kierans__kitchen](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Ddq5-XZtHAo/) · video frames · 15 minutes

## Ingredients

- [ ] Harissa-spiced lamb steaks
- [ ] Flatbread
- [ ] Homemade houmous
- [ ] Rocket
- [ ] Moroccan-style couscous
- [ ] Homemade garlic, lemon & mint yoghurt sauce
- [ ] Pomegranate seeds
- [ ] Fresh mint

## Method

1. Cook the harissa-spiced lamb steaks, then slice them into chunks (they are shown seared outside and pink in the middle).
2. Warm the flatbread.
3. Spread a thick layer of houmous over the flatbread.
4. Top with rocket and the Moroccan-style couscous.
5. Arrange the lamb on top.
6. Drizzle over the garlic, lemon and mint yoghurt sauce.
7. Finish with pomegranate seeds and fresh mint leaves.
